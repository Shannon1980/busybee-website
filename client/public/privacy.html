Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 2026

What we collect

When you sign up for the Busy Bee waitlist, we collect your email address and the source page you signed up from. We use this solely to notify you when Busy Bee is available and to send product updates. We do not sell or share your email with third parties.

Calendar and voice data

Busy Bee processes voice commands on our servers to parse them into calendar events. The text of your voice command and the resulting event details may be stored server-side to provide the service. We do not sell this data or share it with third parties. Calendar data synced through Google Calendar, iCloud, or Outlook is accessed only to create and manage events on your behalf, in accordance with each provider's API terms.

Analytics

We may collect anonymized usage data (crash reports, feature usage) to improve the app. This data cannot be used to identify you.

Retention

Waitlist email addresses are deleted once the relevant launch phase is complete, or upon request. You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing us.
